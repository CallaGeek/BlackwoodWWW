New Home Builds

Your dream home, expertly built

Architectural new builds delivered with care, precision and over 20 years of hands-on experience.

Building a new home is one of the biggest and most exciting projects you'll ever take on — and the builder you choose makes all the difference. At Blackwood Construction we bring over 20 years of experience to every new build, from classic family homes to distinctive architectural designs on challenging and lifestyle sites.

We know that a successful build is about far more than timber and concrete. It's about clear communication, careful planning and having one team take responsibility for the whole journey. That's why we manage all the components of your build and coordinate the architects, engineers and specialist trades needed to bring it together — taking the worry out of the process so you can enjoy watching your home come to life.

As a member of New Zealand Certified Builders and a Licensed Building Practitioner, Boris personally ensures every home we build meets the standard we'd expect of our own.

From first sketch to final handover

Every new build starts with a conversation. We'll talk through your ideas, your section and your budget, and give you honest, practical advice about the best way forward. From there we work alongside you — and your architect or designer if you have one — through pricing, consenting, construction and completion.

Our reputation has been built on referrals from happy clients, and we intend to keep it that way. When we hand over your keys, we want you to be proud of your new home — and glad you chose the team that built it.
