Home Renovations

Breathe new life into the home you love

From single-room updates to whole-home transformations — renovations done properly, with minimal stress.

Sometimes the best home for you isn't a new one — it's the one you already own, reimagined. A well-planned renovation can transform how your home looks, feels and functions, and add real value along the way. At Blackwood Group we bring over 20 years of building experience to renovations of every size across Hawke's Bay.

Renovation work takes a special kind of builder. You're not starting with a blank slate — you're working within an existing structure, often while the family is still living in it. That calls for careful planning, tidy workmanship, honest communication and respect for your home. That's exactly how we work.

We manage all the details of your renovation from start to finish, keeping the project on track and making sure the finished result exceeds your expectations — so the experience is smooth and enjoyable, not stressful.

A renovation experience you'll actually enjoy

We start by listening. What's working, what isn't, and what would make your home perfect? Then we'll give you practical advice on what's achievable within your budget, manage the consents and coordinate every trade — keeping you informed at every step and your home as liveable as possible while we work.

As qualified, friendly builders with NZCB membership and LBP licensing behind us, we deliver renovations with the same care and craftsmanship we'd put into our own homes. The result: a home that feels brand new, without the price tag of starting again.
