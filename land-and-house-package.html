Welcome to The Blackwoods

Welcome to this exclusive lifestyle subdivision in the popular district of Maraekakaho. Your dream home set in park-like grounds, offering plenty of aesthetic appeal, is in a great location only 10 minutes from Hastings or 15 minutes from Havelock North. Set on approx. 1.049 ha with spectacular views sweeping out towards the Ruahine Ranges from its private position, with shared access off Blackwood Lane.

The brand-new development has sensible covenants in place to protect your investment and maintain the integrity of The Blackwoods. The delights of this area are many, with a boutique brewery, wineries and a cafe all within easy reach, and the well-regarded Maraekakaho School a short stroll away. At the entrance, an attractive schist & block feature wall with automatic gate announces the entry to this iconic property.

A standout pavilion-style home

Distinctive architectural features define this standout pavilion-style home. An abundance of double-glazed windows has been a key design feature, bringing nature in and truly making the most of the stunning hillside surroundings and exceptional views — elevating this home to a whole new standard.

Spanning 298.8 m² are spacious rooms connected by polished concrete floors to the kitchen, dining, family room and entrance, with high-quality tiles and carpets gracing the remaining floors. Large living rooms with feature rustic ceilings and light streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows set the perfect scene for dining, entertaining and relaxing. The connections to outdoors spill onto extensive decking areas, with multiple spots to soak in the sun, enjoy the views and dine alfresco-style under the covered area with an outdoor gas fire.

Room for all the family

Four large picturesque bedrooms with a view will cater for all the family. The show-stopping master suite is fit for royalty, with a walk-in robe and luxurious ensuite. Refresh in the tiled walk-in showers, or soak the day away in the deep-set bath.

As for heating and cooling, no expense has been spared — with ducted central heating throughout, plus a gas fire in the family room for ambience.

You can expect download speeds between 100 Mb/s and 200 Mb/s and latency as low as 20 ms via Starlink's high-speed, low-latency broadband internet connection.

Space to grow

The title allows for a possible second dwelling to house the extended family, while still giving you plenty of room for a future implement shed and/or an inground swimming pool.

Two 25,000-litre water tanks capture rainwater for the home, plus access to a shared private water bore for irrigation ensures continuity of supply.
